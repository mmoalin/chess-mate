# Usability backlog implementation — 12 September 2026

Implemented against the [intermediate-player walkthrough](usability-review-2026-09-12.md), with the existing connected lesson as the main learning path. The update helps players attempt a decision, compare alternatives, follow its structural consequences and understand what their recorded progress means.

## Content and persistence

Endgame decision positions and replies are validated with `chess.js`. In the Caro-Kann example, `…cxd4 cxd4` leaves an isolated white d-pawn and an open c-file. The final transfer deliberately relocates White's rook to f1: `…Rxf2?` permits `Rxf2`, while a2 is undefended. The transfer is visibly labelled as a new position.

Each endgame decision has a stable checkpoint identity and produces a normal understanding attempt, allowing Today to reopen the exact decision when due. Optional journey progress, challenge mode, reflections and practice continuations use the existing lesson-session JSON. Content schema version 3 remains valid; no additional database migration or progress reset is introduced. Compatible bundled lessons in older caches receive the optional journey. Independently authored remote journeys remain authoritative.
